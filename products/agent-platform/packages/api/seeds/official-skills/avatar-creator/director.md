## Avatar creator — a real-person presenter, on top of the Avatar creation section in your instructions
- Variety roll first: call roll_avatar_variations once, with the look exactly as Olmo's brief gives it, the gender (or "any" if the brief leaves it open) and the brief's age range exactly as stated (22 to 45 if it gives none — never narrow it yourself; "young adult" or a vibe is not a range). It returns one set of details per variation — age, face shape, skin tone, hair, outfit, place and gesture — all different across the set. Build each prompt from its own set; where Olmo's brief states a detail, the brief wins over the roll.
- Variations: one generate_images call with 4 items, aspectRatio "3:4", no referenceFileIds. The avatar will present UGC video ads, so each prompt describes a real phone-video frame of a creator caught mid-sentence — never a posed portrait or headshot. Write each prompt as labeled lines, in this order, filling every line with concrete specifics:
  Use case: photorealistic-natural
  Asset type: reusable AI presenter avatar for <what Olmo says it is for, e.g. skincare & beauty UGC video ads>
  Primary request: a believable phone-video frame of a <vibe> <look> <gender> creator talking directly to camera, caught naturally mid-sentence rather than posing

  Subject: an exact age; face shape; skin tone; hair length, texture and style; an engaged talking expression caught between words — lips slightly parted and relaxed, not wide open, so the portrait can later be the first frame of a talking video — and eyes looking straight into the lens, never off to the side; one natural hand gesture entering the lower frame; relatable but clearly attractive — a good-looking, well-groomed, camera-ready face with warm, bright, expressive eyes, the kind of real creator brands cast and viewers enjoy watching, never plain or ordinary-looking — with fresh, healthy, even-toned skin with a fresh soft-matte finish — healthy, never shiny — and only subtle real texture with fine baby hairs at the hairline, light natural makeup or a fresh-groomed look, neat brows, healthy hair with natural shine, looking the stated age

  Style/medium: photorealistic candid smartphone front-camera video still, slight phone-camera grain, real skin texture, not editorial — a clean, well-exposed modern phone-camera image, sharp on the face, with real depth: something soft and close in the foreground edge, the person sharp, and the room falling gradually out of focus behind
  Composition/framing: vertical 3:4; chest-up at eye level; arm's-length phone viewpoint with slight handheld asymmetry; gaze into lens.   Lighting/mood: soft natural window or daylight that fits the place, with the energy of the vibe — bright and airy, the light from one named source (the window in the scene) falling softly and flatteringly on the face from one side, with a gentle falloff into soft shadow on the far cheek — never flat, even light from nowhere, never dim, lamp-lit or muddy
